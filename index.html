Research

My research focuses broadly on hadron structure: understanding how the quarks and gluons inside nucleons give rise to emergent properties such as their mass and spin. I am particularly interested in global QCD analysis and the methods used to extract the correlation functions that encode this structure from experimental data and theoretical calculations.

I also study which measurements can provide unique insight into different aspects of nucleon structure and how those measurements can be described phenomenologically and theoretically. One direction of particular interest in my recent and ongoing work is the three-dimensional picture of the nucleon, especially the relationship between transverse-momentum-dependent and collinear descriptions of observables and their underlying correlation functions. The publications and presentations below reflect these interconnected interests.
